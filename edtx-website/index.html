Privacy Policy

Copa do Mundo 2026 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Copa do Mundo 2026 ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses and protects information when you use our iOS application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum data needed to operate the app:

  • Anonymous user ID: A randomly generated identifier created on your device at first launch. Never linked to your name, email or any personal information.
  • Display name: An optional nickname for the prediction leaderboard. You can use the auto-generated name or pick your own. Never tied to your real identity.
  • Predictions: The match outcomes you predict within the app. Stored alongside your anonymous ID to compute leaderboard scores.
  • Purchase status: Whether you have an active Premium subscription, managed by RevenueCat. We never store payment details — all billing is handled by Apple.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your name, email address or phone number
  • Your location
  • Your camera, microphone or contacts
  • Browsing history outside the app
  • Any data from merchandise or ticket links (those open in an external browser)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your prediction picks and points on the leaderboard
  • To process and restore your Premium subscription
  • To improve performance and fix bugs via anonymous crash reports

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase / Firestore — stores anonymous predictions and leaderboard data. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app subscriptions. No payment data is shared with us. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • API-Football (RapidAPI) — provides live match scores. No user data is sent to this service. RapidAPI Privacy Policy

5. Affiliate Links

The Shop tab contains links to third-party retailers (Fanatics, Adidas, FIFA Store, StubHub). When you tap a link, you're taken to an external website. We may earn a small affiliate commission on qualifying purchases. We receive no personal information about your purchases.

6. Data Retention

Your anonymous ID and predictions are stored until you delete the app or request deletion. To delete your data, email us and we will remove your records within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at edtx.dev@gmail.com
