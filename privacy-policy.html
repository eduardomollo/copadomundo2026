Privacy Policy

Copa do Mundo 2026
Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Copa do Mundo 2026 ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses and protects information when you use our iOS application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum amount of data needed to operate the app:

  • Anonymous user ID: A randomly generated identifier created on your device when you first launch the app. This ID is never linked to your name, email or any other personal information.
  • Display name: An optional nickname you may set for the prediction leaderboard. You can leave the auto-generated name or choose your own. This is never tied to your real identity.
  • Predictions: The match outcomes you predict within the app (e.g. "Mexico to win"). These are stored alongside your anonymous ID to compute leaderboard scores.
  • Purchase status: Whether you have an active Premium subscription, managed by RevenueCat. We do not store payment details — all billing is handled by Apple.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your name, email address or phone number
  • Your location
  • Your device contacts, camera or microphone
  • Browsing history outside the app
  • Any data from the merchandise or ticket links (those open in an external browser)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your prediction picks and points on the in-app leaderboard
  • To process and restore your Premium subscription
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs (via anonymous crash reports)

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase / Firestore — stores anonymous predictions and leaderboard data. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app subscriptions. No payment data is shared with us. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • API-Football (RapidAPI) — provides live match scores. No user data is sent to this service. RapidAPI Privacy Policy

5. Affiliate Links

The Shop tab contains links to third-party retailers (Fanatics, Adidas, FIFA Store, StubHub). When you tap a link, you are taken to an external website in your browser. We may earn a small affiliate commission on qualifying purchases. We do not receive any personal information about your purchases.

6. Data Retention

Your anonymous ID and predictions are stored until you delete the app or request deletion. To delete your data, contact us at the email below and we will remove your records within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
